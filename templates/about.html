SeeYouHere.today

SeeYouHere is a small place on the internet — a very boring social network, on purpose.

A small place for lonely people to be alone, together.

Every day, one list: everyone who is here today. Press “I’m here” to join it. Tap a name to say — without words — I see you. They might tap you back.

Your name is yours forever. Your points only go up. Your level only counts the days you’ve shown up.

No feed. No posts. No comments. No messages. No profiles. If you came looking for a pointless website to spend a quiet minute on, this one asks only that you show up.

One Space, one day — resets 08:00 UTC.

Someday this list may hold thousands of names, or just two. Either way, it will be here.

SeeYouHere 是一个被减到几乎什么都不剩的社交空间：每天一张名单，上面是今天到场的所有人。你能做的只有点一下别人的名字，别人也可以点你。没有信息流，没有评论，没有私信——在这个人人都在说话的互联网上，它只留下「在场」本身。有点无聊，有点孤独，也有点安静。
